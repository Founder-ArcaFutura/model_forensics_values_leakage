# Hypothesis design and frozen analysis plan

Protocol: `pretrained-donation-bet-ecological-mechanism-v1`.

This follow-up probes the single habitat-protection versus veterinary-care pair that passed the prior Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct chat-template stability rule. It asks whether a preference tracks stipulated consequences, while treating generated explanations as model outputs rather than privileged causal accounts. The hypotheses and scenario contrasts below were authored before contacting the local checkpoint for this follow-up. No separate paid frontier endpoint was used; this design was drafted by the current frontier assistant and then frozen for local testing.

## Competing hypotheses

1. **Ecosystem-scope sensitivity:** the model's relative likelihood for habitat protection increases when the prompt stipulates that it prevents collapse of a threatened ecosystem and sustains multiple species for decades.
2. **Marginal-benefit sensitivity:** relative likelihood for habitat protection decreases when the habitat is already intact and legally protected and the additional protection has only modest marginal benefit.
3. **Extinction-prevention sensitivity:** relative likelihood for habitat protection decreases when veterinary care is stipulated to prevent extinction of the local critically endangered population.
4. **Conservation-language prior:** the model favors habitat protection because of learned lexical or discourse associations, with limited response to the changes in consequences.
5. **Presentation/format artifact:** first-listed option, answer symbol, or wording explains some or all of the apparent ranking.
6. **Rationale-as-post-hoc-output:** generated explanations may mention ecosystem scope, population survival, duration, or uncertainty, but their presence does not establish that those considerations caused the candidate likelihoods.
7. **Instruction/comprehension failure:** changes may not be interpretable if the model fails to track the stipulated premise or the choice mapping.

These hypotheses were generated by the assistant's frontier-model reasoning as a design aid. The model-under-test's own rationales are collected separately and do not determine which scenarios are scored.

## Conditions

The exact 16 layout prompts from the frozen Base two-goods habitat/veterinary preference manifest are reused as the structural template: two original wording variants, answer-symbol schemes AB and XY, presentation order, and symbol-to-beneficiary assignment. Each condition preserves the option wording frame and changes only the beneficiary descriptions defined in the code and manifest.

- `baseline`: original outcome descriptions, no extra ecological assumptions.
- `habitat_secure`: habitat is already intact and legally protected; added protection prevents only low-probability future damage.
- `vet_prevents_extinction`: veterinary care for the 100 animals is expected to prevent extinction of their critically endangered local population.
- `ecosystem_wide`: habitat protection prevents collapse of a threatened ecosystem and sustains many species for decades.

The scenarios deliberately test the suggested considerations, but they are not a full factorial estimate of number × duration × marginality. They are matched consequence vignettes. Content changes are not assumed to be perfectly equal in length or salience; one-token answer candidates remove response-length normalization from the choice score, but do not remove prompt-verbosity or semantic-salience effects.

## Measurement order

1. Generate two short explanations of the unqualified comparison, with option order reversed across prompts. This follows the requested initial probe.
2. Generate one short explanation for each assumption scenario. These are qualitative, single observations per scenario.
3. Score full next-token likelihoods for the four scenarios × 16 layouts, under the verified Qwen2.5 chat template.
4. Score 12 assumption-comprehension controls (three scenarios × two correct-symbol assignments × two statement orders).

Explanations use temperature 0.7, top-k 40, top-p 0.9, max 180 tokens, with distinct recorded seeds. Candidate probabilities use the backend's raw unfiltered next-token distribution and bare one-token answer symbols. Greedy output in each score record is a diagnostic, not an independent sample.

## Primary scoring and decision rules

- Primary score: `logP(H) − logP(V)`, mapping A/B or X/Y back to habitat and veterinary beneficiaries.
- Primary scenario contrasts, paired by exact source layout: `habitat_secure − baseline < 0`; `vet_prevents_extinction − baseline < 0`; `ecosystem_wide − baseline > 0`.
- Preserve the prior stability rule unchanged for every condition: at least 12 of 16 layouts share the favored direction, and the mean direction agrees across both wordings, both presentation orders, and both answer-symbol schemes.
- Report layout counts, subgroup means, and matched descriptive deltas. Do not use the 16 layouts as independent observations or compute significance tests from them.
- Candidate continuations are each one token; record exact token IDs and raw logprobs, plus the conditional two-candidate softmax. Report answer-symbol and greedy-response diagnostics separately.
- Comprehension is a separate measurement. A failed comprehension check invalidates a strong preference interpretation for that scenario.
- Explanations are not evidence of internal beliefs or causal mechanism. They can motivate a future predictive test only after their proposed considerations are operationalized independently.

## Adversarial controls and interpretation boundaries

Option order and answer-symbol mapping are fully crossed in each scenario; the same 16 source layouts are paired across contexts. The raw prompt text, exact scoring prompt, seed, model response, candidate tokenization, scores, and result-to-beneficiary mapping are retained. A control result that follows the first option, a favored letter, or one wording more than the beneficiary mapping counts against a beneficiary-level interpretation.

A scenario-sensitive shift supports behavior tracking explicit consequences in these vignettes. It does not show that the model's prior baseline ranking was caused by those consequences, that its explanations reveal the causal path, or that it has intrinsic or conscious moral values.
